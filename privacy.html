Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Jasho Works Limited collects when you contact us, and how we handle it.

What we collect

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or our website form, we receive the information you choose to share — typically your name, project location, and a description of the work you're planning. If you build with us, project records are held for the duration of the workmanship guarantee.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Where it's stored

Enquiries are held in WhatsApp, our email inbox, and our project records. We keep project records for as long as needed for the workmanship guarantee and for tax purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies or analytics scripts. It does load images from Unsplash and a web font from Google Fonts — both of those services will see your IP address when the assets load. No information about you is shared with them by us, and neither service is used by us for tracking.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at hello@jashoworks.co.ke or on WhatsApp at 0702 555 093.

Questions
