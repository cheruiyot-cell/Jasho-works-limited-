Kajiado costs

What it costs to build in Kajiado

Kajiado sits slightly above Kiambu and Machakos — water infrastructure and logistics typically add 5–10%. Here are the numbers we actually build to.

3-bedroom bungalow (110–130 m²) KES 6M – 9M
4-bedroom maisonette (180–220 m²) KES 10M – 16M
Full renovation (per m²) KES 22,000 – 50,000 / m²

Excludes plot. Kajiado County approval fees typically add KES 130,000 – 260,000 depending on plot size and zone. Water infrastructure (borehole, tanks, pump) adds KES 350,000 – 700,000 if the plot has no municipal connection.

Kajiado questions

Common questions about building in Kajiado

Do you cover Kitengela, Ngong and Rongai?

Yes — those three plus Isinya, Kiserian, Ngong Town and everywhere in between. Site visits within Kajiado are free.

How long does Kajiado County approval take?

Typically 5–10 weeks. We tell you the realistic range during the quote, not the optimistic one.

Do you handle borehole drilling?

Yes. We coordinate borehole survey and drilling through vetted subcontractors, and install the storage tanks, pressure pump and plumbing ourselves. It's part of the initial scope, not an extra later.

Can you build on a plot I haven't bought yet?

Yes — we'll do a free site assessment before you commit. We'll tell you if the plot has water access issues, drainage problems or access constraints that would drive up the build cost.
