What it costs to build in Nairobi

Nairobi sits at the top of our county range — approval fees and plot logistics are higher here. Here are the numbers we actually build to.

3-bedroom bungalow (110–130 m²) KES 6.5M – 9.5M
4-bedroom maisonette (180–220 m²) KES 11M – 18M
Full renovation (per m²) KES 25,000 – 55,000 / m²

Excludes plot. Nairobi County approval fees typically add KES 180,000 – 350,000 depending on plot size and zone. We tell you the exact figure after the site visit.

Nairobi questions

Common questions about building in Nairobi

Do you cover all of Nairobi County?

Yes. Karen, Lang'ata, Runda, Westlands, Kasarani, Embakasi, Kileleshwa, Kilimani, Dagoretti and everywhere in between. Site visits within Nairobi are free.

How long does Nairobi County approval take?

Typically 6–14 weeks depending on the sub-county and completeness of your plans. We tell you the realistic range during the quote, not the optimistic one.

Can you build on a plot I haven't bought yet?

Yes — we'll do a free site assessment before you commit. We'll tell you if the plot has black cotton soil, drainage issues or access problems that would drive up the build cost.

Do you build in gated communities?

Yes, regularly. We're familiar with the additional requirements most gated estates impose (contractor deposits, working-hours rules, security clearances).
