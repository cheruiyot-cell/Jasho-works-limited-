Budget planning

What it actually costs to build in Kenya

Most contractors won't give you a number until you've sat in their office. Here are the working ranges we build to — so you can plan before you commit a shilling.

3-bedroom bungalow (110–130 m²) KES 5.5M – 9.5M
4-bedroom maisonette (180–220 m²) KES 9M – 18M
1-bedroom rental unit (per unit) KES 2.5M – 4.5M

Excludes plot. Based on standard-to-mid finishes in Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos and Kajiado. Your exact figure depends on the six factors below — and we'll give it to you free in 48 hours.

Per square metre rates (2026)

Basic finishes KES 48,750 – 65,000 / m²
Standard / mid-range finishes KES 65,000 – 90,000 / m²
Premium / high-end finishes KES 90,000 – 122,000+ / m²

Based on current 2026 Nairobi market rates. Excludes land, permits and external works. Rates vary by location within the four counties we serve.

  • 01

    Your plot's shape

    A flat plot is cheapest. A slope means retaining walls; black cotton soil means a deeper foundation. We assess this free on the site visit.

  • 02

    Foundation type

    Strip footing, raft or piling — the right choice comes from a soil test. Skipping that test is the most expensive mistake in Kenyan construction.

  • 03

    Number of floors

    A bungalow costs less per square metre; a two-storey uses less land. Which saves you more depends on your plot.

  • 04

    Roofing material

    Mabati is cheapest upfront. Stone-coated and concrete tiles cost more but cut maintenance for decades. We'll show you the break-even.

  • 05

    Finishes level

    Tiles, cabinetry, sanitaryware and paint are where budgets quietly move. You choose the level — we tell you the number before you choose.

  • 06

    County approvals

    Approval fees, NCA levies and inspections vary by county. We tell you exactly what your plot requires — before you budget for it.

Common cost & timeline questions

How much does it cost to build a 3-bedroom house in Kenya?

Most 3-bedroom bungalows we build in Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos and Kajiado land between KES 5.5M and KES 9.5M, excluding the plot. Maisonettes typically run KES 9M – 18M. Send us your plan or plot details on WhatsApp and we'll give you an itemised figure within 48 hours — free.

How long does it take to build a house?

A standard 3-bedroom bungalow typically takes 4–6 months. A larger maisonette or multi-unit project can take 8–14 months. We give you a realistic timeline with the quote.

How much deposit do you need upfront?

You pay per completed stage — foundation, walling, roofing, finishing — not a lump sum upfront. The first payment covers mobilisation and materials for the foundation only. Every payment is receipted.

Case studies

Three builds, in detail

Challenge, solution, and the numbers we agreed to. No marketing spin.

  • Ruiru, Kiambu

    4-bedroom maisonette on black-cotton soil

    Budget
    KES 12.4M
    Delivered
    11 months
    Finish
    Mid-range

    Challenge. The plot sat on black cotton soil — the kind that swells in the rains and cracks strip footings. Two other contractors had quoted a standard foundation.

    Solution. We commissioned a soil test (KES 45,000, paid by us), confirmed expansive clay, and specified a raft foundation. Absorbed the extra cost within our fixed quote.

    Result. Handed over three weeks ahead of schedule. Two rainy seasons later, zero settlement. The client has since referred three neighbours.

  • Kitengela, Kajiado

    12-unit rental block with no municipal water

    Budget
    KES 28M
    Delivered
    14 months
    Finish
    Standard

    Challenge. The plot had no municipal water connection and the nearest line was 400m away. The landlord's previous contractor had not budgeted for it.

    Solution. We drilled a borehole during mobilisation, added a 10,000L storage tank and a pressure pump, and got the NEMA permits handled. All inside the original quote.

    Result. Fully tenanted within 3 months of handover. Zero water-related tenant complaints in four years.

  • Kikuyu, Kiambu

    Kitchen + 2 bathrooms, family living on site

    Budget
    KES 850K
    Delivered
    5 weeks
    Finish
    Mid-range

    Challenge. A family of five living in the home during a full kitchen and bathroom overhaul. The water had to stay on, and the kitchen had to be usable every evening.

    Solution. Phased work — bathrooms first (one at a time), then the kitchen in a single weekend swap. Temporary sink and cooker set up in the utility room throughout.

    Result. Delivered in 5 weeks, on budget, with the family never once without a working kitchen or bathroom. Client referred two neighbours.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for site visits or quotes?

Site visits and quotes within Nairobi, Machakos, Kajiado and Kiambu are free. For locations further out, we may discuss a small travel fee — but this will always be agreed upfront.

I live abroad. Can you build for me?

Yes — a large share of our clients build from the UK, US, Gulf and Australia. You get a named site supervisor with a direct number, a photo and video update every Friday, and payments released only against completed stages. Many diaspora clients also ask a family member to visit site; we welcome it and will arrange it.

What happens if the project runs late or over budget?

Your quote is fixed. If we missed something in scoping, we absorb it — you don't. On timing, we give you a dated schedule in the quote and send progress every Friday. If we fall behind on our own account, you'll hear it from us before you have to ask.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes. You get a 24-month written workmanship guarantee on all our builds. If our work fails, we return and fix it at our cost.

Can you work with my existing architect or fundi?

Yes. We're happy to work from drawings by your architect, or collaborate with your preferred team. We'll be upfront about how that affects the quote.

Do you handle approvals and paperwork?

We can advise on what's required and recommend trusted professionals for architectural drawings and county approvals. We build to the approved plans.

What areas do you cover?

Nairobi, Machakos, Kajiado and Kiambu counties. If you're just outside these areas, WhatsApp us and we'll let you know if we can reach you.

Can I visit a site you're currently building?

Absolutely — we'd rather you did. WhatsApp us and we'll arrange a visit to a live site near you so you can see the work and speak to the client.
