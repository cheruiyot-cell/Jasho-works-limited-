Machakos costs

What it costs to build in Machakos

Machakos is slightly cheaper than Nairobi and Kiambu — approval fees are lower and land is more available. Here are the numbers we actually build to.

3-bedroom bungalow (110–130 m²) KES 5.5M – 8.5M
4-bedroom maisonette (180–220 m²) KES 9.5M – 15M
Full renovation (per m²) KES 20,000 – 45,000 / m²

Excludes plot. Machakos County approval fees typically add KES 120,000 – 240,000 depending on plot size and zone. We tell you the exact figure after the site visit.

Machakos questions

Common questions about building in Machakos

Do you cover Athi River and Machakos Town?

Yes — both, plus Mlolongo, Syokimau, Kangundo, Wote and everywhere in between. Site visits within Machakos are free.

How long does Machakos County approval take?

Typically 4–8 weeks. Faster than Nairobi or Kiambu in most cases. We tell you the realistic range during the quote.

Can you build on a plot without municipal water?

Yes — a common situation in Athi River and Syokimau. We plan for borehole drilling, storage tanks, and pressure pumps as part of the initial scope, not as an afterthought.

Do you build in Mlolongo and Syokimau?

Yes. Those two areas are among our busiest for rental blocks. We typically have an active site nearby and can arrange a visit.
