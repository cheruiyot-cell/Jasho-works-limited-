What it costs to build in Kiambu

Kiambu sits in the middle of our range — cheaper than Nairobi, more accessible than Kajiado. Here are the numbers we actually build to.

3-bedroom bungalow (110–130 m²) KES 5.5M – 8M
4-bedroom maisonette (180–220 m²) KES 9M – 14M
Full renovation (per m²) KES 22,000 – 48,000 / m²

Excludes plot. Kiambu County approval fees typically add KES 150,000 – 280,000 depending on plot size and zone. We tell you the exact figure after the site visit.

Kiambu questions

Common questions about building in Kiambu

Do you cover all of Kiambu County?

Yes. Ruiru, Juja, Thika, Kikuyu, Limuru, Githurai, Kiambu Town and everywhere in between. Site visits within Kiambu are free.

How long does Kiambu County approval take?

Typically 4–10 weeks depending on the sub-county. Ruiru and Juja tend to be faster than Kikuyu and Limuru. We tell you the realistic range during the quote.

Can you build in a gated estate in Kiambu?

Yes, regularly. We're familiar with the additional requirements most gated estates impose in Ruiru, Juja and Kiambu Town — contractor deposits, working-hours rules, security clearances.

Do you build in Juja and Ruiru specifically?

Yes — those are two of our busiest areas. We typically have an active site in one of them, and we're happy to arrange a visit so you can see the work in progress.
